# Changelog

## 1.0.0

The first version that is complete enough to hand to a mud engineer.

### The mud check

- reads the check sheet with everything past the well, the date, the depth and
  the weight optional, because a check taken while tripping has a weight and a
  funnel and nothing else
- all three rheology models, scored against every reading that was taken rather
  than argued about
- alkalinity split three ways, chlorides, hardness, methylene blue, sand, the
  filter cake and high temperature filtration with the spurt pulled out of it
- retort solids split between what was bought and what came out of the hole
- the invert emulsion readings: oil to water ratio off the liquid phase alone,
  electrical stability as a trend rather than a number, water phase salinity
- the mud balance checked against water and against its own retort

### Hydraulics

- the annulus split at every point where the hole or the string changes, so the
  slowest length is found rather than averaged away
- velocities, Reynolds numbers, friction factors and pressure losses one length
  at a time, with the surface equipment as an equivalent length of drill pipe
- bit nozzles, horsepower per square inch, impact force, and the nozzle set for
  either criterion
- circulating density with the cuttings in it, and the margins either side
- swab and surge, and the fastest the pipe can be moved inside them
- slip velocity through Moore's three regimes, and the transport ratio

### The system

- capacities, pump output, circulating times, slugs, trip sheets, pit volumes
  off sloped and cylindrical tanks, transfers, and the buoyancy the mud gives
  the string
- weight up, cut back, blend, dilute, pills and sweeps, and what a mixing order
  takes through the hopper
- solids control as a train, with the sizes carried forward from stage to stage
- the mud side of a kill sheet, cross checked before it is used

### Around the job

- the mud programme, section planning, casing seats and the loss ladder
- product stock that reconciles, reordering, transfers between rigs, purchase
  orders and the stock count
- cost per foot, the invoice, the estimate and the spend against the budget
- the daily report, the morning report, the section report, the end of well
  recap and the crew change handover
- doctor, which reads the whole file set together and reports where it
  contradicts itself
